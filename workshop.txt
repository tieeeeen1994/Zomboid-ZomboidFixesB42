title=Zomboid Fixes B42.20
description=A collection of fixes for Build 42.20, mostly for multiplayer.
description=
description=[h2]Admin and debug fixes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fast item transfers[/b] - With Fast Timed Actions on, moving items is quick in multiplayer, like it is in single player.
description=[*][b]Animal gender[/b] - Changing an animal's gender in the animal info window now changes the animal, and sticks for everyone instead of reverting. Vanilla only flips a hidden flag, so a sow made male keeps the sow model, the sow texture and no mate of her own; the animal is swapped to the matching type instead, so it looks and breeds like what it now is.
description=[*][b]Foraging debug menu[/b] - Adding, moving and refreshing forage icons now works, and icons you add can actually be picked up.
description=[*][b]Item editor[/b] - Edits now save for items anywhere: crates, vehicles, the floor or another player's inventory.
description=[*][b]Debug Add Fluid[/b] - Adding fluid to a rain collector or any other fluid container in the world with the debug menu now really fills it. Vanilla emptied it on the server instead and never told anyone, so the water only seemed to be there until you looked again.
description=[*][b]Fast Move falls[/b] - Fast Move can carry you over stairs faster than the game puts you on the steps, leaving you in the air until you drop, often to your death. With Fast Move on you now glide down to the floor without taking damage, without needing God Mode, which would also heal every wound and clear every moodle. Off by default; turn it on in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Admin tag[/b] - The red admin tag now shows for every admin panel cheat, not just some of them. A sandbox option can hide it completely.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Gameplay fixes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Negative weight items[/b] - Items with negative weight, which let players carry or store more than they should, are deleted automatically. Can be turned off in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Broken clothing[/b] - Clothing torn apart while worn is dropped for everyone, not just on your own screen, so it no longer comes back undamaged when you relog. Off by default; turn it on in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Metal shin armor run speed[/b] - Vanilla had the run speed of Metal Shin Armor and Articulated Metal Shin Armor the wrong way round, so the articulated ones were slower. The values are swapped back.
description=[*][b]Chickens lost in hutch nest boxes[/b] - A hen laying an egg gives up her place inside the hutch while she sits in the nest box, so another bird can walk in and take it. If every place is taken when she finishes, the game deletes her instead of putting her back, and you just see a chicken that vanished. She is caught and put back instead. Off by default; turn it on in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Hutch dirt speed[/b] - Vanilla rolls for nest box dirt once per laying hen every frame, roughly eighty times as fast as the hutch floor beside it, so a busy coop goes from clean to filthy in a couple of minutes. Both counters can be slowed to any share of normal, down to never. Set to 1 (vanilla) by default; change it in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Stair railings[/b] - Running or jogging into a low fence vaults it automatically, and the game never checks what is on the other side, so brushing the railing of a staircase or a stairwell sends you over it and down a floor. Railings on or beside stairs no longer auto vault; ordinary fences on flat ground still do, and a railing can still be climbed on purpose by stopping and pressing interact. Off by default; turn it on in sandbox options.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Other mods[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Guns of Marz attachment tooltips[/b] - The game lists every weapon an attachment fits on a single tooltip line, and a tooltip is only ever as narrow as its widest line, so once Guns of Marz has added thirty guns a scope or a laser throws a banner across the screen. The list is rebuilt under its heading and wrapped to a line length of your choosing. Off by default; set the line length in sandbox options.
description=[/list]
