title=Zomboid Fixes B42.20
description=A collection of fixes for Build 42.20, mostly for multiplayer.
description=
description=[h2]Admin and debug fixes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fast item transfers[/b] - With Fast Timed Actions on, moving items is quick in multiplayer, like it is in single player.
description=[*][b]Animal gender[/b] - Changing an animal's gender in the animal info window now sticks for everyone instead of reverting.
description=[*][b]Foraging debug menu[/b] - Adding, moving and refreshing forage icons now works, and icons you add can actually be picked up.
description=[*][b]Item editor[/b] - Edits now save for items anywhere: crates, vehicles, the floor or another player's inventory.
description=[*][b]Admin tag[/b] - The red admin tag now shows for every admin panel cheat, not just some of them. A sandbox option can hide it completely.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Gameplay fixes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Negative weight items[/b] - Items with negative weight, which let players carry or store more than they should, are deleted automatically. Can be turned off in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Broken clothing[/b] - Clothing torn apart while worn is dropped for everyone, not just on your own screen, so it no longer comes back undamaged when you relog. Off by default; turn it on in sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Metal shin armor run speed[/b] - Vanilla had the run speed of Metal Shin Armor and Articulated Metal Shin Armor the wrong way round, so the articulated ones were slower. The values are swapped back.
description=[*][b]Chickens lost in hutch nest boxes[/b] - A hen laying an egg gives up her place inside the hutch while she sits in the nest box, so another bird can walk in and take it. If every place is taken when she finishes, the game deletes her instead of putting her back, and you just see a chicken that vanished. She is caught and put back instead. Off by default; turn it on in sandbox options.
description=[/list]
description=
